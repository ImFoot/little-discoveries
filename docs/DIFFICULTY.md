# Difficulty after player rejection

The earlier 70% challenging / 30% easy quota and fifty new scenes were rejected by the intended player. They are not the current brief. Labels, valid polygons and successful automatic collection did not establish enjoyable play.

Current reference: scene 24, The floating garden. Keep its clear recognizable object identities and warm detailed illustration. The three samples aim only a little harder: spread targets over different heights and side nooks, use depth and modest partial overlaps, and keep most of each recognizable silhouette visible. Do not substitute tiny fragments, visual ambiguity or damaged previews for concealment.

Samples 51–53 contain twelve targets each, below the permanent maximum of fifteen. All use tight unchanged picture crops instead of alpha-masked cutouts. The enlarged view shows the same real pixels and overlaps. Objects have ordinary names; outlines and generous taps remain independent of preview cropping.

Review all three on the intended iPhone before commissioning another batch. Judge recognition first: after seeing the preview, is it clear what object is wanted, and after reveal does the real object match? Then judge placement/difficulty relative to 24. Record concrete problematic objects rather than replacing art wholesale or relying on a numeric challenge label.
